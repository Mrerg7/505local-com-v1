Buying a premium domain should feel boring and secure. Here is the process used for **505Local.com**.

## Step 1 — Submit a serious inquiry

Email [sales@desertrich.com](mailto:sales@desertrich.com) or use the offer form with:

- Your name and best contact email
- Intended use (platform, local business brand, investment, etc.)
- Offer amount in USD
- Any timing constraints

You receive a confidential reply within 24–48 hours.

## Step 2 — Agree terms and open escrow

Once price is agreed in writing, both sides open a transaction with a licensed escrow provider such as Escrow.com (or Dan.com / attorney escrow if preferred). Funds are deposited and held — the domain does not move until escrow confirms payment is secured.

## Step 3 — Transfer and release

505Local.com is registered with Cloudflare. Transfer options:

- **Registrar push** when both parties use compatible accounts
- **Auth-code transfer** to the buyer’s preferred registrar

Most transfers complete within **1–3 business days** after funds clear. Escrow releases payment to the seller only after the buyer confirms control of the domain.

## What you receive

The sale covers the domain name itself. Demonstration website content is separate unless included by written agreement.

## Buyer tips

- Prefer escrow over informal wire-only deals
- Confirm the whois / registrar account before releasing funds
- Plan DNS cutover so email and site go live on your schedule
